# Libryo

> Libryo (ERM Libryo) is a regulatory compliance management platform for Environmental,
> Health and Safety (EHS) teams. It automates legal registers, streams site-specific
> regulatory obligations across jurisdictions, and tracks legislative change so operators
> know which laws apply to each of their sites. Founded in 2016, now part of ERM.

## What Libryo does

Libryo maps regulatory obligations to specific operating sites. A "Libryo" is a filtered,
site-specific stream of the legislation that applies to one operation or location. Products
include Libryo Sites (site-specific EHS regulatory intelligence), Libryo Assess (assessment
and audit management), Libryo Collaborate, and custom legal registers. Customers are
concentrated in manufacturing, energy and utilities, chemicals, mining, logistics and
telecoms. Libryo also supplies regulatory content as a data-as-a-service feed into
third-party GRC platforms.

## API surface

Libryo operates a partner-facing RESTful API. Access is partner-gated: there is no
self-service developer signup, and client credentials are issued by Libryo directly. The
full endpoint reference is provided to partners under agreement and is NOT public. What is
public is the OAuth 2.0 integration guide.

- Base URL: https://api.libryo.com/api/v1
- OAuth guide: https://github.com/libryo/libryo-api-oauth-docs
- Authorization endpoint: https://my.libryo.com/oauth/authorize
- Token endpoint: https://api.libryo.com/api/v1/oauth/token
- Grant type: authorization_code (three-legged OAuth 2.0)
- Versioning: URI path, currently v1

### Scopes

- `view-legal-report` — view the authorizing user's compiled register of applicable legal obligations
- `search-legislation` — search the Libryo legislation corpus on the user's behalf
- `list-libryos` — list the site-specific regulatory streams available to the user

Scopes are requested space-delimited on the authorization request. All published scopes are
read-only; no write scopes are documented.

### Errors

Errors from the OAuth endpoints follow RFC 6749 §5.2 as `application/json`, with a
non-standard `hint` field naming the offending parameter:

    {"error":"invalid_request","error_description":"...","hint":"Check the `client_id` parameter"}

Libryo does NOT use RFC 9457 problem+json. On the redirect URI, rejection is signalled as
`?error=access_denied` and success as `?code=<authorization-code>`.

## Important caveat for automated crawlers

`*.libryo.com` is a wildcard DNS record pointing at the platform load balancer in AWS
eu-west-1. ANY hostname under libryo.com — including `developer.libryo.com` and
`docs.libryo.com` — answers HTTP 200 with the application login page. These are NOT
developer surfaces. Do not treat a 200 on an invented subdomain as evidence of a
documentation or developer portal.
